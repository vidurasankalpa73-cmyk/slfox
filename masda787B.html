MASDA RX-7

*SIGNIFICANCE: The Mazda RX-7 is a famous Japanese sports car, well known for its lightweight design and rotary engine.

ENGINE: Rotary engine. The most famous high-performance version, the RX-7 FD, used a 1.3L twin-turbocharged twin-rotor engine

POWER: Around 252 hp in the later RX-7 FD models, depending on the market and version.

WEIGHT: Around 1,250 kg, depending on the model and specification.

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES: Famous for its rotary engine, pop-up headlights, lightweight body, excellent handling, and sporty design.

PRODUCTION: The RX-7 was produced from 1978 to 2002 in three generations: SA/FB, FC, and FD.

MASDA RX-8

*SIGNIFICANCE: The Mazda RX-8 is a famous Japanese sports car, known for its unique rotary engine and four-door design.

ENGINE: 1.3L naturally aspirated RENESIS twin-rotor rotary engine.

POWER: Around 238 hp in the high-performance version.

WEIGHT: Around 1,300–1,400 kg, depending on the model.

DISTINCTIVE FEATURES: Famous for its rotary engine, rear-hinged “freestyle” doors, four-seat layout, sporty handling, and unique design.

PRODUCTION: Produced from 2003 to 2012.
